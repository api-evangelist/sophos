Sophos CEO Joe Levy notes that of roughly 359 million businesses worldwide, fewer than 35,000 employ a CISO, yet most cybersecurity frameworks and products assume a senior security leader is in place. The piece explores how AI might help bridge this strategic capability gap for organizations without dedicated security leadership.
