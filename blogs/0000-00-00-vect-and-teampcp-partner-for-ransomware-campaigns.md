Two cybercriminal groups, TeamPCP and Vect, announced a formal operational partnership in late March 2026, combining TeamPCP's credential harvesting and data theft expertise with Vect's ransomware deployment capabilities. The alliance leverages supply chain attacks to target multiple organizations across various sectors.
